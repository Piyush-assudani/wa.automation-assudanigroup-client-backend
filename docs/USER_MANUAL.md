# User Manual — Assudani WA Automations

## 1. Getting Started
1. **Sign In:** Open the web portal and log in with your assigned username and password. Use the eye icon in the password field to verify your input if needed.
2. **Channel Setup (`Settings`):**
   - **Company Channel:** Uses the platform's managed WhatsApp Business number.
   - **Custom Meta API:** Enter your own Meta Phone Number ID, WABA ID, and Permanent Access Token.

## 2. Managing Contacts
1. Navigate to **Contacts**.
2. Click **Create Empty List** or **Upload New Sheet** (`.csv` or `.xlsx`).
3. The system automatically detects columns (`Name`, `Phone`, `City`) and updates your list in real time.

## 3. Media & Templates
1. **Media Library:** Upload images or videos (`MP4`). Uploaded assets are stored on the server and synced with Meta when needed.
2. **Templates:** Create new templates (including image or video headers) or sync existing approved templates from your Meta WABA.

## 4. Launching Campaigns
1. Open **Campaigns -> Launch Campaign**.
2. Enter a Campaign Name, choose an approved Meta Template, attach header media if required, and select one or more Contact Lists.
3. Choose **Send Immediately** or **Schedule for Later**.
4. Your wallet balance is checked before launch and deducted per sent message. Any message rejected by Meta is automatically refunded to your wallet.

## 5. Live Chat & Billing
- **Live Chat:** View and reply to inbound WhatsApp messages from your contacts in real time.
- **Billing:** Track your current wallet balance and itemized debit/refund ledger. Contact your Master Admin to recharge funds.
